Yuun is a Chinese almanac (huangli) for everyday life: daily Good/Avoid activities, solar terms, lunisolar dates, and culture from classical sources.

TODAY
- Today home with week strip, stem-branch, Good/Avoid, and optional For you from birth
- Month calendar (solar, lunar, solar terms, festivals)
- Culture library in Settings

FOR YOU
- Add one birth profile for a daily For you summary
- Deeper personal reads arrive in a later update

FAMILY
- Family lunar or solar birthday reminders (up to 3)
- Morning push and optional evening heads-up

NOT
- Not Western astrology or horoscopes
- Not predictive

PRIVACY
Your birth profile personalizes the daily For you summary. Delete account in Settings. No tracking ads.
