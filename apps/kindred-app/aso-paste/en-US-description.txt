Yuel is a BaZi (Four Pillars) couples-chart tool - a relationship typology grounded in classical Chinese cosmology and modern AI. Not Western horoscope. Not predictive matchmaking. Not a compatibility score.

WHAT IT IS

- Two birth charts side by side: Day Masters, Five Elements balance, and classical interaction patterns (combination, clash, harm, penalty)
- A cultural overlay - like an MBTI-pair study: tendencies and friction themes, not a verdict or prediction
- AI chat that references classical BaZi concepts when you ask about your pair
- Private invite link: they add their own birth; you never see their raw details from the link alone

WHAT IT IS NOT

- Not Western astrology, horoscopes, or zodiac signs
- Not fortune-telling or a pass/fail match score
- Not a generic compatibility quiz
