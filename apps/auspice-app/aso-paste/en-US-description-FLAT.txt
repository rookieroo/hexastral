Yuun is a Chinese almanac (huangli) for everyday life: daily Good/Avoid activities, solar terms, lunisolar dates, and culture from classical sources. Today: the home has a week strip, stem-branch, Good/Avoid, and an optional For you from birth; the public almanac is free for everyone, every day; tap the chevron under the week strip for the month calendar (solar, lunar, solar terms, festivals); Settings open from the icon in the home header; and a culture library lives in Settings. For you: add one birth profile for a daily For you summary; deeper personal reads and timeline tools arrive in a later update. Family: lunar or solar birthday reminders, plus a morning push and an optional evening heads-up. Not Western astrology or horoscopes, and not predictive. Privacy: your birth profile personalizes the daily For you summary and syncs to your account only when multi-device is on; delete account in Settings; no tracking ads.
