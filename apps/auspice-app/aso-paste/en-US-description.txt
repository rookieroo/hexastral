Yuun is a Chinese almanac (huangli) for everyday life: daily Good/Avoid activities, solar terms, lunisolar dates, and culture from classical sources.

TODAY
- Today home with week strip, stem-branch, Good/Avoid, and optional For you from birth
- Free Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen, and Apple Watch complications
- Swipe right for month calendar (solar, lunar, solar terms, festivals)
- Swipe left for Settings
- Culture library in Settings

FOR YOU
- Add one birth profile for daily For you (summary on Free; detail on Pro)
- Personal Ba Zi and Zi Wei deep-read, timeline, and what-if on Pro

FAMILY
- Family lunar or solar birthday reminders
- Morning push and optional evening heads-up

NOT
- Not Western astrology or horoscopes
- Not predictive

FREE vs PRO

Pro 4.99 USD/month or 39.99 USD/year: full For you reasons, timeline, deep-read, unlimited family reminders.
