Yuun is a Chinese almanac (stem-branch / yi-ji / solar terms) for cultural reference. Not fortune-telling. Not medical advice.

Sign-in is NOT required for the daily public almanac, widgets, or calendar.
Sign-in is only needed at the subscription / Pro step (Sign in with Apple).

How to test Free:
1. Launch the app. Browse Today, calendar, culture library without an account.
2. Optional: add on-device birth info to see For you summary.

How to test Pro (Sandbox):
1. Open any Pro feature or the paywall.
2. Sign in with Apple.
3. Purchase with an App Store Sandbox tester account (device: Settings - App Store - Sandbox Account).
4. Restore Purchases works if needed.

No ads. No tracking.
